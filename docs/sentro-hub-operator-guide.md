# Sentro Hub Operator Guide

## Weekly Rhythm

### Monday

- Check the admin dashboard for new requests, pending leave, and unread announcements.
- Review attendance from the previous working day and correct any missing or obviously broken hours.
- Check contractor onboarding, contract expiry, and document request queues.

### Midweek

- Review incoming requests and time-off submissions.
- Update announcements or SOPs if there are policy, scheduling, or campaign changes.
- Check project and client assignment pages to confirm active work is staffed correctly.

### Payroll Window

- Review `Attendance` and confirm billable hours are correct.
- Open `Payroll` and review each contractor payout draft.
- Approve payouts only after disputes, overtime, and adjustments are final.
- Use `Payouts` and `Invoice Log` to keep payment status aligned with what was actually sent.

### End Of Week

- Review pending contract signatures and document requests.
- Check project payment progress and contractor payout status for active project-based work.
- Post a short announcement for next week if staffing, deadlines, or holidays changed.

## Core Pages

- `Dashboard`: fast triage for the week.
- `Attendance`: fix hours before payroll.
- `Time-Off`: approve, reject, or escalate leave requests.
- `Requests`: manage internal team asks that are not leave-related.
- `Payroll` and `Payouts`: finalize compensation.
- `Projects`: monitor client revenue, costs, and contractor cuts.
- `Documents`: contracts and signatures.
- `Clients`: who is assigned where.
- `Announcements` and `SOP`: operational communication and internal reference.

## Good Operating Habits

- Keep `Attendance` clean daily so payroll does not become a once-per-cutoff fire drill.
- Use `Announcements` for decisions the whole team needs to see, not just Slack.
- Add notes when rejecting leave, editing hours, or adjusting pay so the audit trail stays useful.
- Keep contractor assignments current whenever projects or retainers change hands.
- Treat the demo seed as demo-only and replace it with real users before production use.
